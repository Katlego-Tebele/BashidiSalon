Badishi Beauty Salon

Badishi Beauty Salon & Beauty Supply is a proudly South African beauty destination based in Randburg, Johannesburg. We believe every woman deserves to look and feel extraordinary — and we're here to make that happen.

From premium 13×4 SDD Pixie Curly Frontal Wigs to flawless nail sets and relaxing pedicures, we take every detail seriously. Our goal is simple: to give you a beauty experience that leaves you feeling confident, beautiful, and ready to own your crown.

We welcome walk-ins and bookings via WhatsApp. Come as you are — leave looking like the best version of yourself.
